The pipeline

Evidence is gathered by code; the model only ever gets to read text that was really fetched, and only ever answers inside a closed vocabulary. The two grey steps are where a human was needed.

01

Seed and search

Five fixed queries per app plus the brief's doc hint. First-party docs ranked above blogs.

02

Fetch corpus

Up to seven pages pulled through Composio's Firecrawl tool, cached to disk so reruns cost nothing.

03

Registry lookup

MCP status resolved against the Glama registry. The model is told the answer rather than asked — its own MCP knowledge goes stale in weeks.

04

Constrained synthesis

One call. Closed enums, and every claim must carry a verbatim excerpt from the corpus.

05

Mechanical validation

No model. Is the excerpt really in that page? Is the URL one we fetched? Do verdict, tier and blocker contradict each other?

06

Targeted repair

Failures become new search queries. A repair is only accepted if it is strictly less broken. Bounded at two attempts.

H1

Human: the obscure ones

Apps with thin or absent public docs. The agent's instinct is to fill the gap; a person has to look and say "there is nothing here".

H2

Human: the sample

Twenty apps, two per category, read by hand against the real docs. This is the accuracy number below.

How much of this is true

A stratified sample of the 100 was checked by hand against live docs and scored field by field. Both passes were kept on disk so the two can be compared.
